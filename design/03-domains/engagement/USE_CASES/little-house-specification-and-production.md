## Part I: Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ — Specification & Purpose

### Section 1: What is Little House? (Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ là gì?)

**Definition:**

Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ is a **collection/combination of Buddhist scriptures** (tổ hợp các bài kinh điển Phật giáo) designed for specific spiritual purposes.

**Spiritual Context:**

Humans have two types of ailments:
1. **Physical ailments** (bệnh thân) — medical conditions
2. **Spiritual ailments** (bệnh linh tính) — caused by karmic debt from sentient beings (spirits, ghosts, aborted fetuses, etc.)

**Origins of Spiritual Ailments:**

Karmic creditors may attach to a person:
- **Deceased family members** (tổ tiên, người thân đã mất)
- **Aborted fetuses** (thai nhi bị sảy hoặc phá bỏ)
- **Former close friends or acquaintances** (bạn bè thân thiết)
- **People with unresolved karmic debts** (người từng có oan kết)
- **Spirits residing in the home** (linh tính trong nhà)

**Consequences of spiritual ailments:**
- Physical illness
- Easy irritability / mood swings
- Declining fortune (vận khí suy giảm)
- Setbacks in career, education, relationships
- Family discord (gia đình bất hòa)
- Household items frequently breaking/malfunctioning

**Historical Context:**

Master Lü Junhong (Lư Quân Hoằng Đài Trưởng), a renowned Buddhist teacher in Australia, under the guidance of Guanyin Bodhisattva, received the dharma of Little House with great spiritual penetration (đại thần thông). Master Lü teaches sentient beings to:
- Trust in Buddhism (tin Phật)
- Recite sutras to change destiny (niệm kinh để cải biến vận mệnh)
- Liberate from suffering to attain joy (lìa khổ được vui)
- **Cross over spiritual creditors** (siêu độ linh tính)
- **Cure spiritual ailments** (trị bệnh linh tính)
- **Cross over deceased relatives** to better realms

**Spiritual Power:**

Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ is a **supreme dharma treasure** (đại pháp bảo tối thượng) granted by Guanyin Bodhisattva in this era to:
- Cross over spiritual creditors (siêu độ linh tính)
- Eliminate karmic obstacles (tiêu trừ nghiệp chướng)

**Efficacy Comparison:**

In the spiritual realm, Little House is like a **large bank note/check** (tấm chi phiếu lớn):
- **Old custom:** Burning paper money to deceased — equivalent to small change in the spiritual realm
- **Little House:** Energy equivalent to **tens of thousands of currency units** in spiritual realm → incomparably greater efficacy

**Benefit:** Little House is specifically used to clear karmic debts owed to spirits (nợ nần từ linh giới), which cannot be avoided — they must be repaid. Little House provides the "energy" (năng lượng) to settle these debts. Response (linh ứng) is extremely evident.

### Section 4: Types/Scenarios of Little House Use (Các Loại Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ)

**Type 1: Little House for Karmic Creditors** (Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ cho Người Cần Kinh — Karmic Creditors)

**Context:**

Most suffering and misfortune in life stem from **karmic debts** (nghiệp nợ) from past lives:
- Bad karma created in the past comes to be repaid at a certain time in this life
- This causes suffering (thọ báo)
- Karmic creditors (oan gia trái chủ / spirits) come to collect payment

**Solution:**

After burning Little House to cross over these creditors, they depart. Many problems are resolved.

**Approach if creditor is unknown:**

If you don't know specifically who the creditor (Người cần kinh) is, you can:
- Recite Little House for **general karmic creditors** (hồi hướng chung cho Người cần kinh)
- "Người cần kinh" = respectful term for spiritual creditors
- In the spiritual realm, karmic law operates: creditors who are owed will receive payment according to spiritual law (địa phủ đều có âm luật, không duyên không đến, không nợ không đến)

---

**Type 2: Little House to Cross Over Deceased Relatives** (Siêu Độ Người Đã Mất)

**Purpose:**

Cross over ancestors and deceased loved ones to **better realms** (cảnh giới tốt hơn).

**Precaution:**

If you dream of a deceased person, you must recite Little House for them. Otherwise, you will encounter misfortune (gặp điều không thuận).

---

**Type 3: Little House for Aborted/Miscarried Fetuses** (Siêu Độ Thai Nhi Bị Sảy Hoặc Phá Bỏ)

**Context:**

Aborted or miscarried fetuses have great difficulty being reborn (rất khó được đầu thai). They:
- Wander in the human realm (lang thang nơi dương gian)
- Linger around family members (quanh quẩn bên người thân)

**Consequences for family:**

- Physical illness (thân thể không khỏe)
- Children disobedient (trẻ nhỏ không nghe lời)
- Family discord (gia đình bất hòa)
- Career setbacks (sự nghiệp không thuận), etc.

**Solution:**

Little House can cross over these fetuses. Once these spirits depart, many family problems are resolved.

---

**Type 4: Little House for Household Spirits** (Siêu Độ Linh Tính Trong Nhà)

**Context:**

Many households have spirits causing:
- Household items frequently malfunctioning (đồ điện dễ hư)
- Plumbing/utilities frequently clogging (đường ống tắc nghẽn)
- Poor health of family members (người nhà sức khỏe kém)
- Family discord (gia đình bất hòa), etc.

**Solution:**

Little House can cross over household spirits.

**Extension to businesses:**

Similarly, shops, offices, and companies with spirits experience:
- Poor business (kinh doanh không thuận)
- Career setbacks (sự nghiệp không suôn sẻ)

Solution: Use Little House to cross over business spirits.

---

**Type 5: Little House to Resolve Karmic Entanglements** (Hóa Giải Oan Kết)

**Context:**

- Family discord (gia đình bất hòa)
- Poor neighbor relations (quan hệ láng giềng không tốt)
- Workplace conflicts (mâu thuẫn giữa đồng nghiệp), etc.

**Approach:**

If reciting **Karmic Bond Resolution Mantra (Giải Kết Chú)** alone shows minimal efficacy:
- Combine with Little House for **Karmic Creditors** (Người cần kinh)

Usually, both Little House types should be recited together.

**Type 6: Reserve Little House** (Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ Dự Trữ)

**Context:**

If practitioner has capacity to recite many Little House sheets.

**Recommendation:**

Beyond daily recitation quota, keep a **reserve supply** (dự trữ):
- Store in **red envelopes** or **red cloth** (phong bì đỏ hoặc vải đỏ)

## Part II: In Ấn — Printing & Production Standards

### Section 1: Downloading Template (Tải Xuống)

**Official Source:**

Log into Master Lü Junhong's blog:
- **URL:** [http://lujunhong2or.com](http://lujunhong2or.com)
- **Navigation:** Home page → Left sidebar → "Kinh Văn Và Bản Chỉnh Lý" (Scriptures and Corrected Versions)
- **Link:** Click "Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ – Mẫu Chuẩn" (Little House – Standard Template)
- **Download:** Download the electronic file

**Printing Material:**

Use **A4 yellow paper** (giấy A4 màu vàng) to print.

---

### Section 2: Printing Specifications (In Ấn)

**Paper Requirements:**

- **Size:** A4 format
- **Color:** Yellow (both sides of paper are yellow)
  - **Preferred:** Lemon yellow (màu vàng chanh) — BEST
  - **Acceptable:** Golden yellow (màu vàng kim) — Second choice
  - ✅ Can use lemon yellow or similar yellow tones
  - ❌ CANNOT use: pale yellow, orange-yellow, yellow-green, or other shade variations

- **Print:** One side only

**Non-Compliant Color Specifications (INVALID):**

❌ If template has lemon yellow BUT size is too small → **INVALID**
❌ Golden yellow is acceptable, BUT if layout is wrong → **INVALID**
❌ If layout is correct BUT color is pale yellow → **INVALID**
❌ If layout is wrong → **INVALID**
❌ Orange-red color even if layout is correct → **INVALID**

**Effective Date:**

- **From 2016 onwards:** ALL non-compliant Little House shapes are **ineffective** (không được sử dụng) and will affect efficacy (ảnh hưởng đến hiệu quả)

---

### Section 3: Cutting Specifications (Cắt)

**Yield per A4 sheet:** One A4 sheet → **4 Little House sheets** (bốn Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ)

**Shape:** Each sheet is a **rectangle** (hình chữ nhật)

**Dimension Spec:**
- **Black border frame size:** 9.1 cm × 13.95 cm
- **Tolerance:** ±0.5 cm (5 mm) allowed

---

### Section 4: Quality Control Standards (Quy Chuẩn)

**Printing Quality:**

Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ must be:
- **Clear and complete** (rõ ràng, hoàn chỉnh)
- Printed according to **official standard template** from blog
- **NO arbitrary changes** (không được tùy ý thay đổi)
- Non-compliance affects efficacy (ảnh hưởng đến hiệu quả)

**Specific Standards (Must All Be Met):**

| Standard | Requirement | Invalid If |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Circles** | Must be clear, solid | Contains obvious black dots |
| **Circle shape** | Perfect circles | Oval/elliptical shapes |
| **Circle spacing** | Circles separate | Circles touching/connected |
| **Recitation count label** | No "共" (total) character | Contains "共" character below numbers |
| **Scripture title** | Single column | Split into two columns |
| **Border** | Frame border complete | Border cut off |
| **Character type** | Traditional Chinese** (繁體) | Simplified Chinese (簡體) |
| **Font/Layout** | Exact specification match | Font, position, spacing, circle size, spacing all must match exactly |
| **Border/circles** | All must be closed/complete | Gaps in outline |
| **Print quality** | Fully visible | Parts faint or unclear |

**Correction Allowed:**

If printing error causes unfilled outline or unclear circles:
- ✅ Can use **black pen** to supplement and complete

**Critical Error:**

❌ **Little House with "共" character = INVALID** (cannot be used)

## Part III: Operational Procedures

### Section III: Materials Needed When Reciting Little House (Vật Phẩm Cần Thiết Khi Niệm)

**Essential Materials:**

| Item | Purpose | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| **Kinh thư (Sacred texts)** | Reference for four mantra types to recite | Keep near Little House during recitation |
| **Bộ đếm (Prayer beads/counter)** | Count mantras recited | Helps track progress accurately |
| **Bút đỏ (Red pen)** | Mark circles after completing mantras | Red signifies spiritual energy |
| **Bút đen (Black pen)** | Fill in title, name, date | Administrative/record-keeping |
| **Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ chưa sử dụng** | Blank/unused Little House | Must be new, never used |
| **Phong bì đỏ (Red envelope)** | Store completed Little House | Preserves spiritual energy |
| **Tấm vải đỏ (~2 thước / 60cm+)** | Cloth base during marking | Must be red fabric, minimum 60cm × 60cm |

**Proper Handling During Recitation:**

- Little House must be placed on red cloth when marking circles
- Treat with reverence at all times (tôn trọng, trang nghiêm)
- Do not fold, bend, or damage the paper
- Keep in clean environment (bedroom, bathroom not suitable)

---

### Section IV: Materials Needed When Burning Little House (Vật Phẩm Cần Thiết Khi Đốt)

**Essential Burning Materials:**

| Item | Purpose | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| **Nhíp (Tweezers/tongs)** | Grip Little House safely when burning | Preferably new/clean |
| **Đĩa hoặc chậu sứ (Ceramic dish/bowl)** | Collect ash after burning | White, plain ceramic only; no metal; NEW, never used |
| **Diêm hoặc bật lửa (Matches/lighter)** | Ignite Little House | Avoid Buddha lamp if in proximity to altar |
| **Khăn giấy/phong bì (Tissue/envelope)** | Wrap ash after burning | Ready before starting; ash cannot be discarded randomly |

**Strict Requirements:**

- **Container must be ceramic** — never metal bowls or pans
- **Container must be white and plain** — no patterns, decorations, or colored glazes
- **Container must be NEW** — never previously used for food or other purposes
- **Once used for ash, never use for food again**
- Tools (tweezers, dishes) should NOT be left openly near altar; wrap in red cloth and store carefully

---

### Section VII: Ash Disposal (Xử Lý Tro Của Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ)

**Proper Ash Disposal Protocol:**

1. **Wrap ash immediately** — Use clean tissue or standard envelope to wrap ash after cooling
2. **Never mix with garbage** — Do NOT discard with common trash, especially:
   - Food waste with strong odors (fish, meat, etc.)
   - Dirty items
3. **Safe disposal method** — Combine with:
   - Paper scraps/waste paper
   - Can be disposed with regular recyclables
4. **Optional:** Dispose in nature if appropriate and respectful:
   - Near water (river, ocean) — ash floats away naturally
   - In garden soil — returns ash to earth
   - Never in polluted areas

**Spiritual Respect:**

- Ash contains the spiritual essence of mantras recited
- Wrapping shows respect (tôn kính) and prevents energy loss
- Never treat ash as common garbage

---

### Section VIII: Storage and Preservation (Bảo Quản Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ)

**Storage for Partially Recited Little House:**

- **Must be wrapped** in red envelope or red cloth immediately
- **Purpose:** Prevent energy loss (tránh thất thoát năng lượng)
- Can be stored safely in any clean location

**Storage for Completed Little House:**

- **Must be in red envelope** or wrapped in red cloth
- **Ready for burning** — keep accessible but protected

**Storage for Unused Little House:**

- **Also wrap in red cloth** — even blank sheets must be preserved with respect
- **Do not fold or bend** — keep sheets flat
- **Keep in clean location** — not in:
  - Bedroom (sleeping area associated with intimate activity)
  - Bathroom (contaminated area)
  - Kitchen (cooking area with strong smells)

**Preservation Principles:**

- Treat Little House with reverence (tôn kính bảo quản cẩn thận)
- Little House contains sacred mantras — handle as sacred object
- Red color (đỏ) signifies spiritual protection and energy containment
- Proper storage ensures efficacy when finally burned

---

### Section IX: Handling Damaged or Invalid Little House (Xử Lý Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ Bị Hủy)

**Damage Scenarios That Require Disposal:**

| Condition | Severity | Action Required |
|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| **Wrong information filled in** | High | Must be invalidated and replaced |
| **Contaminated/soiled** | High | Must be invalidated and replaced |
| **Contains "共" character** | Critical | MUST be destroyed immediately — never burn |
| **Printing errors affecting form** | Medium | Can be corrected with black pen if circles only |
| **Tearing or major damage** | High | Must be invalidated |

**Proper Invalidation Protocol:**

**Step 1:** Create replacement Little House
- Transfer count of mantras already recited to NEW blank Little House
- Use black pen to re-mark circles with recitation count

**Step 2:** Inform Bodhisattva (bạch với Bồ Tát)
- Recite: "Quán Thế Âm Bồ Tát đại từ đại bi, con [tên] đã làm sai Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ, nay xin hủy bỏ."
- Translation: "Great Compassionate Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva, I [name] have made a mistake with this Little House and now ask to invalidate it."

**Step 3:** Dispose of damaged Little House
- **For non-critical damage:** Use pen to strike through title and dedicant name
  - Fold into small pieces
  - Wrap in paper or envelope
  - Discard with regular waste

- **For "共" character (CRITICAL):** Destroy immediately
  - This version is completely invalid
  - Do not burn (contains structural error)
  - Wrap and discard as regular waste

**Important Notes:**

- Do NOT burn invalidated Little House (except in specific ritual circumstances)
- Always prepare replacement BEFORE invalidating current one
- The spiritual energy of invalidated Little House is transferred to replacement
- This ensures continuity of merit (gộp công đức vào Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ mới)

## Notes for AI/Codegen

1. **Specification Enforcement:** Little House is NOT flexible like other practices. ALL production standards are mandatory. Non-compliance = complete inefficacy.

2. **Paper Color Validation:** System must enforce yellow-only colors. Consider:
   - Color picker UI with pre-defined yellow swatches
   - RGB/HSL value range validation for "yellow"
   - Visual preview comparison tool

3. **Dimension Tolerance:** 9.1 × 13.95 cm ± 0.5 cm is the standard. Consider:
   - Dimension input fields with unit conversion (cm ↔ inches)
   - Visual ruler overlay to verify cutting
   - Manual measurement entry with tolerance check

4. **Quality Checklist:** 9 mandatory checks (circles, spacing, characters, title format, border, font, font properties, print clarity). UI must show all 9 with checkboxes.

5. **Post-Printing Correction:** If circles are unfilled due to printing error, allow **black pen supplementation** but NOT correction of structural errors (layout, characters, title columns, etc.).

6. **Critical Error: "共" Character:** Any Little House containing "共" (total character) is completely invalid. System must strongly reject with red error state.

7. **Localization:** All Vietnamese text must use full diacritics (Á, À, Ả, Ã, Ạ, etc.).

8. **Integration with Burn Workflow:** After validation passes, Little House is ready for burning (refer to related documents: validate-little-house-burn-conditions.md, etc.).
