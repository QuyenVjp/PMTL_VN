## Purpose

Kinh văn tự tu (personal scripture copying/storage) is a **long-term merit accumulation practice** distinct from daily recitation (công khóa) and Little House practice (Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ):

| Practice | Analogy | Purpose | Storage |
|----------|---------|---------|---------|
| **Công khóa** (Daily) | Daily food/living expenses | Daily energy replenishment | Recorded immediately |
| **Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ** | Debt repayment | Karmic debt settlement | Burned immediately |
| **Kinh văn tự tu** | Retirement savings/pension | Merit accumulation for afterlife | **Stored for burning at death** |

**Critical distinction:** Kinh văn tự tu numbers are **COMPLETELY SEPARATE** from daily practice and Little House counts. No mixing or consolidation.

## What is Personal Scripture Storage?

### Core Concept

**Kinh văn tự tu** = copying/reciting mantras onto specially formatted paper templates and **accumulating them over time** for eventual burning at time of death.

**Purpose:** Before dying, the practitioner burns all accumulated scriptures to:
1. **Self-transcendence** — help oneself ascend beyond the Three Realms (Tam Giới) or into higher paths (Tam Thiện Đạo)
2. **Merit activation** — accumulated merit becomes a spiritual vehicle at time of death
3. **Bodhisattva support** — merit stored in documents creates evidence of cultivation

**Why accumulate?** When facing karmic obstacles at death (which nearly everyone does), having mountains of accumulated merit documents provides:
- Spiritual testimony of lifelong dedication
- Karmic offset at critical moment
- Greater probability of favorable rebirth

## Creating Personal Scripture Storage

### Materials

| Item | Specification | Notes |
|------|---------------|-------|
| **Paper** | Yellow paper (printed template) | Same color as Little House (lemon or golden yellow) |
| **Template** | Dedicated personal scripture format | Size/dimensions not critical (unlike Little House) |
| **Pen (black/blue)** | For filling name and dedication info | Black or blue ink |
| **Pen (red)** | For marking completion dots | Red ink only |
| **Red envelope/cloth** | For storing completed sheets | Preserve spiritual energy |

### Filling Out Scripture Template

**Dedication block ("Kính tặng"):**
- Use black or blue pen
- Write **recipient's name** (own name or another person's name)
- At gender field (thiện nam / tín nữ): strike through inappropriate gender, write full name after

**Name considerations:**
- If changed name after formal name-change ceremony before Bodhisattva → use new name
- Otherwise → use birth/current registered name

Request blessing from Bodhisattva:

>
> (Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva with great compassion and great mercy, I [name] now recite personal scripture copy 'YYY', requesting Bodhisattva to bear witness.)

Or general blessing:
> "Xin Quán Thế Âm Bồ Tát đại từ đại bi gia hộ cho XXX [name in dedication] được bình an, thân thể khỏe mạnh..."

### Marking Completion

| Step | Tool | Rule |
|------|------|------|
| **Each repetition completed** | Red pen | Mark one red dot |
| **When sheet is full** | Black/blue pen | Write date (day/month/year) |
| **Then wrap** | Red paper/cloth | Wrap completed sheet immediately |
| **Timing rule** | ⚠️ **STRICT** | Red marking must be **before midnight**. Latest: 23:59:59 same day |

### Timing Restrictions by Scripture Type

| Scripture | Timing Rule |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Chú Đại Bi** | Anytime (day/evening OK) |
| **Tâm Kinh** | ❌ **NO recitation after 22:00** |
| **Vãng Sanh Chú** | ❌ **NO recitation after 22:00** |
| **Lễ Phật Đại Sám Hối Văn** | ❌ **NO recitation 22:00–05:00** |
| **All types** | ❌ **Red dot marking NOT allowed 22:00–05:00** (next day marking OK) |

**Note:** If recitation extends past 22:00, mark the dot next morning before using scripture for anything else.

## The 8 Types of Personal Scripture Storage

### 1) Chú Đại Bi (Great Compassion Mantra)

**Purpose:** Grow fundamental life energy; open channels of advancement.

**Daily typical:** Up to 21 recitations per sheet (can exceed)

**Accumulation note:** This + Tâm Kinh are like hydrogen gas — essential base energy.

**Death usage:** Burned at time of death to strengthen ascending spirit.

---

### 2) Tâm Kinh (Heart Sutra)

**Purpose:** Develop wisdom; open spiritual intelligence channels.

**Daily typical:** Up to 21 recitations per sheet

**Recitation restriction:** ⚠️ **No copying after 22:00**

**Death usage:** Burned to illuminate path upward.

---

### 3) Lễ Phật Đại Sám Hối Văn (88 Buddhas Great Repentance)

**Purpose:** Eliminate major karmic obstacles; remove heavy spiritual chains blocking ascension.

**Daily typical:** Up to 21 recitations per sheet (not exceed without reason)

**Important metaphor:** If Chú Đại Bi (hydrogen) is gas filling a balloon, and karmic obstacles are iron weights below the balloon:
- Even infinite hydrogen won't lift a balloon with too many iron weights
- Must remove the iron weights (karmic obstacles) first
- At death moment, **many copies of Repentance scripture burn = major obstacle removal = easy ascension**

**Risk warning:** This scripture can activate karmic spirits. If pain/discomfort appears during recitation → burn 4–7 Little Houses immediately (separate from scripture recitation count).

**Recitation restriction:** ⚠️ **No copying 22:00–05:00**

---

### 4) Vãng Sanh Chú (Rebirth Mantra)

**Purpose:** Transcend small spirit debts (insects, animals consumed lifetime); resolve lingering attachments.

**Daily typical:** Up to 21 recitations per sheet

**Recitation restriction:** ⚠️ **No copying after 22:00**

**Death usage:** Help small spirits resolve debts so they don't pull down ascending spirit.

---

### 5) Chuẩn Đề Th神Chú (Cundi Mantra)

**Purpose:** Accelerate merit accumulation speed; gain favorable circumstances.

**Daily typical:** Up to 21 recitations per sheet

**Death usage:** Speed up ascension; smoothen path.

---

### 6) Công Đức Bảo Sơn Thần Chú (Virtue Treasury Mantra)

**Purpose:** Consolidate and gather all merit from lifetime; create concentrated merit reserve.

**Daily typical:** Up to 21 recitations per sheet

**Death usage:** At moment of death, ignite massive merit reserve accumulated.

**Special note:** Can also recite for unborn babies (in womb) or children <5 years to transfer their past-life merit to current life.

---

### 7) Kinh A Di Đà (Amitabha Buddha Sutra)

**Purpose:** If goal is Pure Land rebirth (Tây Phương Cực Lạc), this scripture serves as **formal petition to Amitabha Buddha to receive you**.

**Daily typical:** 1+ recitations per sheet

**Death usage:** Amitabha comes to escort ascending spirit to Pure Land.

**Prerequisite:** Only create this type if Pure Land rebirth is explicit goal.

---

### 8) Chú Tiêu Tai Cát Tường (Auspiciousness Mantra)

**Purpose:** Prevent calamities before they manifest; resolve emerging obstacles.

**Daily typical:** Up to 21 recitations per sheet

**Critical timing:** ⚠️ **MUST burn BEFORE problem manifests**
- If dreaming of many snakes → burn prophylactically
- If sensing ominous signs coming → burn now
- **Do NOT wait until crisis arrives** — like putting out fire only after house burns

**Recitation frequency:** Begin 3–4 days before anticipated crisis, 1–2 sheets per week maximum.

**Risk warning:** Burning too many sheets of this mantra can attract spirit attention → **don't overdo it**. Untangling Mantra (Giải Kết Chú) has same risk — not recommended as personal scripture type.

## Using Personal Scripture Storage

### Normal Usage: Burning at Death

**Primary purpose:** Before death (when possible), personally burn accumulated scriptures to:
1. Activate lifetime merit
2. Transcend beyond Three Realms or into higher path
3. Create strongest possible spiritual condition at death moment

**Burning method:** Same as Little House (ceramics dish, new tweezers, wrap ash respectfully).

**Timing:** Best done in daytime; if evening, not after 23:59 for Tâm Kinh and Vãng Sanh Chú.

### Emergency Usage: Using Before Accumulation Complete

**Scenario:** Major illness, surgery, important exam, critical deadline approaching.

**Protocol:**
- Can withdraw completed scripture sheets for urgent use
- Example: Major illness → burn completed **Chú Đại Bi** sheets immediately
- Before surgery → burn **Chú Đại Bi**, **Tâm Kinh**, **Chuẩn Đề Thần Chú** sheets
- Before important exam → same as surgery protocol

**Timing:** ⚠️ **Burn 3–4 days BEFORE the event, not day-of**
- If burning day-of → effectiveness drops significantly
- Better: burn week before, 1–2 sheets maximum
- If advance notice → can start month before, 1–2 sheets weekly

---

### Converting Scripture Storage to Little House

**Option:** Transfer accumulated merit from personal scripture to Little House form.

**Conversion formula:**
```
If one sheet of personal scripture (type X) = N recitations
Then convert to: N ÷ 27 sheets of Little House (same type mantra in LH)
```

**After conversion:**
- Fold the completed personal scripture sheet into small pieces
- Wrap in paper
- Discard as regular waste

## Important Restrictions & Warnings

### Lễ Phật Đại Sám Hối Văn (Repentance Scripture)

**Daily limit:** Maximum 21 recitations per sheet

**Reason:** Over-accumulating can trigger too many karmic spirits simultaneously at death → create chaos instead of smooth ascension.

**Only exception:** At death moment itself — burn all accumulated sheets (unlimited).

---

### Giải Kết Chú (Untangling Mantra)

**NOT recommended as personal scripture storage type**

**Reason:** This mantra is **target-specific** (requires naming specific person to untangle from). Burning many sheets at once without clear targets can attract problematic spirit attention → create complications.

**Alternative:** Recite fresh daily as needed in daily practice, don't accumulate.

---

### General Accumulation Wisdom

**Too much of one type can backfire:**
- Each scripture addresses different obstacle categories
- Balanced portfolio (mix of Compassion + Wisdom + Repentance + Merit) is safer
- Never over-accumulate any single type without reason

**Recommended balance at death:**
- Chú Đại Bi: High quantity (foundation)
- Tâm Kinh: High quantity (wisdom)
- Lễ Phật Đại Sám Hối Văn: Moderate quantity (too much risks activation)
- Vãng Sanh Chú: Moderate quantity (completion/cleanup)
- Chuẩn Đề Thần Chú: Moderate quantity (acceleration)
- Công Đức Bảo Sơn Thần Chú: Moderate quantity (consolidation)
- Kinh A Di Đà: As needed (Pure Land goal only)
- Chú Tiêu Tai Cát Tường: As needed (emergency only, not routine)

## Business Rules

### Timing Enforcement

| Rule | Enforcement | Consequence |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Red dot marking must complete before midnight | Backend timestamp validation | Warning if after 23:59; prevent retroactive marking |
| Heart Sutra: no copying after 22:00 | UI disable + backend block | Block recitation logging, show "Too late — try tomorrow" |
| Repentance: no copying 22:00–05:00 | UI disable + backend block | Same as above |
| Emergency burn: min 3–4 days before event | Validation on form submission | Show warning if user burns day-of, recommend earlier |
| Emergency burn: max 1–2 sheets per week | Tracking counter | Warn if exceeding accumulation depletion rate |

### Accumulation Integrity

| Rule | Storage | Tracking |
|------|---------|----------|
| No mixing types in count | Separate ledgers per type | Each type maintains own recitation sum |
| No mixing with daily practice | Completely isolated counter | Daily practice count ≠ scripture storage count |
| No mixing with Little House | Separate bucket entirely | Little House ÷ 27 conversion only at explicit request |
| Completed sheets stay accumulated | Archive after logging | Can only withdraw for emergency use (explicit action) |

## Notes for AI/Codegen

1. **Three separate counting systems** (must isolate):
   - Daily practice (công khóa): immediate
   - Little House: debt repayment
   - Personal scripture storage: long-term accumulation
   - **Never consolidate or mix these counts**

2. **Time-based restrictions** (strict enforcement):
   - Heart Sutra/Rebirth after 22:00 → BLOCK
   - Repentance 22:00–05:00 → BLOCK
   - Red marking after 23:59 → WARN → allow next-day marking
   - Emergency burn timing: logic remind but don't block (user judgment)

3. **Emergency vs. routine** (mutually exclusive workflows):
   - Routine: log → accumulate
   - Emergency: request permission → warn about timing → burn subset of accumulated sheets
   - After emergency burn → decrement accumulation counter

4. **Conversion path** (explicit user action):
   - Only trigger conversion when user explicitly requests "convert to Little House"
   - Formula: sheet recitations ÷ 27 = equivalent Little House sheets
   - Destroy original sheet after conversion

5. **Practitioner-specific journey** (personalization):
   - Different practitioners may emphasize different scripture types
   - System should surface imbalances (e.g., "You have 300 Compassion but only 20 Wisdom")
   - Recommend balanced accumulation without forcing

6. **Localization:** All Vietnamese text must use full diacritics (Á, À, Ả, Ã, Ạ, etc.)

7. **Integration points:**
   - User profile → practitioner name
   - Emergency event calendar → warn on burn timing
   - Daily practice tracker → show separation visually
   - Little House system → conversion mechanism
