## Philosophy & Social Impact of Buddhist Teaching

### "White Speech Buddhist Teachings" (Bạch Thoại Phật Pháp)

**Definition & Purpose:**
Organic combination of traditional human spirit values + Buddhist philosophical essence. Uses simple, concise language to explain:
- From righteous practice in human realm → liberation in Dharma realm
- Integrates with people's daily spiritual practice and social contribution
- Teaches renunciation of evil, cultivation of good, self-purification, benefit to others

**Core Teaching Method:**
- Begins from fundamental practices: filial piety, ceremony/propriety
- Advances through Five Precepts and Ten Virtues
- Progresses to karma understanding and cause-effect wisdom
- Leads to mental liberation and Dharma realm understanding
- Combines Buddhist and Confucian humanistic values

**Social Integration Strategy:**
- Raises individual moral self-awareness and practice capacity
- Corrects social atmosphere and values through long-term gradual influence
- Addresses core modern moral crisis: absence of faith/belief
- Provides spiritual foundation for socialist core values
- Bridges traditional culture revival with contemporary society needs

### Role in National Cultural Strategy

**National Significance:**
- Implements central government guidance on traditional culture promotion
- Contributes to socialist core values cultivation (富強、民主、文明、和諧 for national level; 自由、平等、公正、法治 for society; 愛國、敬業、誠信、友善 for citizen)
- Addresses the "Nixon Warning" (1999): necessity of preventing ideological weakening through cultural foundation
- Counteracts negative cultural invasion and value confusion in transitional society
