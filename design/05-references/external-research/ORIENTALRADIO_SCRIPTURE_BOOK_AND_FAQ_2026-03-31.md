## PHẦN 2 — FAQ DÀNH CHO NGƯỜI MỚI

> Source: `https://orientalradio.com.sg/en-faq/`
> Title: "Frequently Asked Questions – For Beginners"
> Ngày crawl: 2026-03-31

---

### Q1 — What to do with the offerings on the Buddhist Altar before you go on a vacation?

Before leaving for a short duration:
- Refresh the Great Compassion Water
- Put up fresh flowers and fruits (ensure they will not rot before you return)
- Leave the altar uncovered
- Photograph the altar (without incense burning), wrap the photo in red cloth, bring it with you
- Offer Heart Incense (Tâm Hương) morning and evening while away
- If conditions allow at your destination, offer real incense

---

### Q2 — What to do if you are going to relocate your Buddhist Altar?

**At the old location**:
1. Make an incense offering; wait until it finishes
2. Wrap all items in red cloth/materials

**At the new location**:
1. Position statues/images on the altar first
2. Make incense offerings with three sticks
3. Recite the Great Compassion Mantra 7 times and Heart Sutra 7 times
4. Make full prostrations
5. Recite a prayer inviting Guan Yin Bodhisattva to protect and bless the family
6. **Relocate the altar before moving other household belongings**

*Note*: No additional blessing ceremony needed for the statues when relocating.

---

### Q3 — Knowledge about offering incense, grand incense and incense burner

**Regular incense**:
- Offer morning and evening at consistent times
- Optimal morning times: 6 am, 8 am, 10 am
- Evening times: 6 pm, 8 pm, 10 pm
- Regular days: one incense stick per statue, or use one burner for the entire altar
- Special dates (lunar 1st/15th, Guan Yin's Birthday, Enlightenment Day, Renunciation Day): offer three sticks

**Grand incense (sandalwood)**:
- Light sandalwood using an oil lamp flame
- **Never blow out sandalwood** — let it burn naturally
- Sandalwood can be reused by laying it horizontally beside the incense burner

---

### Q4 — Quantity of Oil Lamps to be placed on a Buddhist Altar

- Match the number of lamps to statues or images
- A pair of lamps or one lamp for the whole altar is acceptable
- For single Buddha/Bodhisattva statue: one lamp is best; two is also acceptable
- Red candles should be offered in pairs

**Important rules**:
- Extinguish lamps and candles after prostrations or **before incense finishes burning**
- Burning lamps without incense attracts foreign spirits
- Electrical lamps may supplement real lamps but should not run 24/7
- Order to turn off: candles first → oil lamps → electric lights
- **Never blow them out — use a lid instead**
- Prolonged electrical lighting invites foreign spirits

---

### Q5 — Will recitation attract foreign spirits?

Karmic creditors seek repayment according to spiritual laws. The Little Houses method allows practitioners to repay karmic debts, eliminating calamities and transforming destiny.

Without repayment through recitation, debts manifest as illness, misfortune, or accidents.

Physical soreness after reciting the Eighty-Eight Buddhas Great Repentance indicates activated karmic obstacles transforming into spirits — beneficial for immediate resolution rather than future major illness.

*Key advice*: Begin repayment before problems arise rather than waiting until serious conditions develop.

---

### Q6 — What to do if you are changing your name?

"Generally, you should use the name that is written on your birth certificate, as it would be the name that is recognized by the spiritual world."

However, if a new name has been used for **over one year**, it gains spiritual effect. In that case:
- Lodge a Name Change Application
- Use the new name for reciting Little Houses

---

### Q7 — Can you Kneel before the Altar during Menstruation Period?

"It is absolutely alright for females to perform their Daily Recitation, as well as to recite and burn Little Houses during menstruation."

- Recite during sunny daytime when possible
- Stop if discomfort arises
- After showering: make incense offerings, perform prostrations, and burn Little Houses

---

### Q8 — Feeling Sleepy while doing Recitation?

**Causes and guidance**:
- Physical exhaustion: avoid reciting in this state as effectiveness decreases
- Sleepiness during specific sutras: may indicate good recitation performance and body recharging
- Sleepiness with Heart Sutra or Amitabha Pure Land Rebirth Mantra: recite during daytime or sunny weather when yang energy peaks
- If accompanied by headaches or dizziness: karmic creditors may be requesting debt repayment
- Otherwise: use willpower to persist through sleepiness and practice vigorously

---

### Q9 — How to Keep our Buddhist Scriptures Booklet?

- Keep booklets in **clean places**, handled by **clean hands**
- Do not fold them
- Do not place below belly level
- Do not store near bathrooms or inappropriate materials
- Booklets with Buddha/Bodhisattva images: store **flat facing upward**
- Booklets with only scriptures (no images): may be kept upright

---

### Q10 — How to Offer Heart Incense?

**Method**:
1. Visualize Guan Yin Bodhisattva before you
2. Imagine offering an oil lamp
3. Take and light incense (visualized)
4. Join palms, raise incense above eyebrows between the eyes
5. Visualize placing incense in the pot
6. Make full prostrations
7. Silently state your prayers

**When to use**:
- Those without home altars
- During travel or business trips
- On holidays

**Important restriction**: Only practice heart incense if you maintain a daily altar with regular offerings to Buddhas and Bodhisattvas at home. Images on computer screens or printed materials lack the energy field of properly-tended altars — offerings to these reach all surrounding spirits, creating risks.

---

### Q11 — Can One Recite Without Having A Buddhist Altar at Home?

Using Heart Incense (as described in Q10), one can recite without a home altar.

- Offer heart incense before recitations
- "You can only make heart incense offerings if you have an altar at home where you pay respects and make offerings to Buddhas and Bodhisattvas everyday."
- Other images (phone screens, printed materials) lack required energy fields; offerings to them involve risks

---

### Q12 — Must One Recite in front of A Buddhist Altar?

With memorized sutras and mantras, recitation is possible:
- During chores
- While driving
- At work

**The environment should be clean.** Avoid reciting in:
- Kitchens with killing or meat preparation
- Toilets
- Basements
- Dirty places

In other locations, seek forgiveness from Guan Yin Bodhisattva before beginning.

---

### Q13 — About Daily Recitation (Công Khóa Hằng Ngày)

**Daily Recitation comprises fixed daily quotas**:
- Great Compassion Mantra: **3 times**
- Heart Sutra: **3 times**
- Eighty-Eight Buddhas Great Repentance: **1 time**
- Cundi Dharani: **21 times**

> "Performing Daily Recitation is as important as daily food intakes, and it is like earning an income for daily living expenses."

**Little House recitations** resemble loan repayments — separate from Daily Recitation:
- Daily Recitation and Little House mantras **cannot be counted together**
- Separate prayers and separate counting are required

---

### Q14 — About Little House (Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ)

**Two illness types exist**:
1. **Medical illnesses** — treated by doctors
2. **Spiritual illnesses** — medically unexplainable, caused by:
   - Deceased relatives
   - Close friends who have passed
   - Aborted/miscarried children
   - People from past conflicts
   - Household spirits

Extended spiritual occupation causes: physical illness, bad temperament, misfortune, career/study difficulties.

> "In the Age of Dharma Decline, with the recitation of Little Houses, we are able to eliminate our karmic obstacles due to negative karma, and transfer merits to foreign spirits and help them to proceed to a better place."
